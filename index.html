📜 Underdog Football League Rules

• Weekly Pick: Choose 1 game from NFL or FBS CFB. Wins must be outright upsets to gain point spread as points. Covering spread means nothing.

• Double Shocks (DS): 3 DS allotted for the entire season. Winning doubles the spread. If the underdog loses, the point spread is subtracted from participant total.

• Deadlines: Picks must be locked before Friday 8:00 PM (or kickoff for weekday / TNF games).

• Postseason Mini-Tournaments: Bowl-pocalypse (6 non-NY6 bowls) + The NIL Invitational (CFP round-by-round picks + National Championship tiebreaker).
